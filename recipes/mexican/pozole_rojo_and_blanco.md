# Pozole Rojo (Mexican Red Pozole)

- Preparation Time: 25 minutes
- Cooking Time: 3 hours (Total Time: 3 hours 25 minutes)
- Serving Size: 8 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Soup

- Pork shoulder, 1.5 kg (3.3 lb), cut into large chunks
- Pork ribs, 500 g (1.1 lb)
- Water, 3 liters (12 cups)
- White onion, 1 large, halved
- Garlic cloves, 6
- Bay leaves, 2
- Salt, 2 tsp (or to taste)

### For the Red Chilli Sauce

- Dried guajillo chilli, 6
- Dried ancho chiles, 3
- Garlic cloves, 3
- Ground cumin, 1 tsp
- Dried oregano, 1 tsp
- Water, 1 cup (240 ml)

### For the Pozole

- Hominy (canned or cooked), 800 g (28 oz), drained and rinsed

### Garnishes

- Shredded lettuce or cabbage, 2 cups
- Radishes, 6, thinly sliced
- White onion, 1/2 cup, finely diced
- Fresh cilantro, 1/2 cup, chopped
- Lime wedges, for serving
- Dried oregano, for sprinkling
- Tortilla chips or tostadas, for serving

## Method

1. Place the pork shoulder, pork ribs, water, onion, garlic, bay leaves, and salt in a large stockpot.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 2–2.5 hours, skimming off any foam that rises to the surface.
3. While the pork cooks, remove the stems and seeds from the dried chiles.
4. Soak the chiles in hot water for 15–20 minutes until softened.
5. Blend the softened chiles with garlic, cumin, oregano, and 1 cup of water until smooth.
6. Strain the chile sauce through a fine-mesh sieve for a smoother broth.
7. Remove the cooked pork from the pot and shred it into bite-sized pieces.
8. Discard the onion, garlic, and bay leaves from the broth.
9. Return the shredded pork to the pot and add the hominy.
10. Stir in the red chile sauce and simmer for an additional 30–40 minutes.
11. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt as needed.
12. Serve hot in bowls topped with lettuce, radishes, onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of fresh lime juice.

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve with warm tostadas spread with refried beans.
- Add sliced avocado for extra richness.
- Sprinkle additional dried oregano over each bowl.
- Pair with Mexican rice or fresh corn tortillas.
- Offer hot sauce on the side for extra heat.

# Pozole Blanco (Mexican White Pozole)
- Preparation Time: 20 minutes
- Cooking Time: 3 hours 30 minutes (Total Time: 3 hours 50 minutes)
- Serving Size: 8 servings

## Ingredients 

### For the Soup

- Pork shoulder, 1.5 kg (3.3 lb), cut into large chunks
- Pork ribs or pork neck bones, 500 g (1.1 lb)
- Water, 3 liters (12 cups)
- White onion, 1 large, halved
- Garlic cloves, 8
- Bay leaves, 2
- Salt, 2 tsp, or to taste

### For the Pozole

- Hominy (canned or cooked), 800 g (28 oz), drained and rinsed

### Garnishes

- Shredded lettuce or cabbage, 2 cups
- Radishes, 6–8, thinly sliced
- White onion, 1/2 cup, finely diced
- Fresh cilantro, 1/2 cup, chopped
- Lime wedges, for serving
- Dried oregano, for sprinkling
- Crushed red pepper flakes or chile powder, optional
- Tortilla chips, tostadas, or warm corn tortillas, for serving

## Method 

1. Place the pork shoulder, pork ribs, water, onion, garlic, bay leaves, and salt into a large stockpot.
2. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
3. Skim off any foam that rises to the surface during the first 20–30 minutes.
4. Cover partially and cook for 2.5–3 hours, or until the pork is tender and easily pulls apart.
5. Remove the pork from the broth and shred it into bite-sized pieces.
6. Discard the onion, garlic, and bay leaves from the broth.
7. Return the shredded pork to the pot.
8. Add the hominy and simmer for another 30 minutes to allow the flavors to combine.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning with additional salt if needed.
10. Ladle the pozole into bowls and allow each person to add their preferred garnishes.

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve with shredded cabbage, radishes, diced onion, cilantro, and fresh lime juice.
- Accompany with crispy tostadas topped with refried beans.
- Offer dried oregano and chile powder at the table so guests can season to taste.
- Pair with warm corn tortillas and sliced avocado.
- Serve alongside a fresh Mexican salsa for added flavor.
