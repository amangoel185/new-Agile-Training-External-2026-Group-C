# Birria Tacos

- Cuisine Type: Mexican
- Preparation Time: 30 minutes
- Cooking Time: 3 hours 30 minutes (Total Time: 4 hours)
- Serving Size: 6 servings (approximately 12 tacos)


## Ingredients

### For the Birria Meat
- Beef chuck roast, 1.5 kg (3.3 lb), cut into large chunks
- Beef short ribs, 500 g (1.1 lb)
- Salt, 2 tsp
- Black pepper, 1 tsp

### For the Chilli Sauce
- Dried guajillo chilli, 6
- Dried ancho chilli, 3
- Dried árbol chilli, 2 (optional, for extra heat)
- Roma tomatoes, 3 medium
- White onion, 1 medium, roughly chopped
- Garlic cloves, 6
- Beef broth, 4 cups (960 ml)
- Apple cider vinegar, 2 tbsp
- Ground cumin, 1 tsp
- Dried oregano, 1 tsp
- Ground cinnamon, ¼ tsp
- Bay leaves, 2

### For the Tacos
- Corn tortillas, 12
- Oaxaca cheese or mozzarella, 300 g (10 oz), shredded
- Fresh cilantro, ½ cup, chopped
- White onion, ½ cup, finely diced
- Lime wedges, for serving


## Method 

1. Remove the stems and seeds from the dried chiles. Toast them in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
2. Place the toasted chiles in hot water and soak for 15–20 minutes until softened.
3. Roast the tomatoes, onion, and garlic in a skillet or under a broiler until lightly charred.
4. Blend the soaked chiles, roasted vegetables, beef broth, vinegar, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, and a little salt until smooth.
5. Season the beef with salt and pepper. Place it in a large pot or Dutch oven and pour the blended sauce over the meat. Add the bay leaves.
6. Simmer gently for 3–4 hours (or cook in a pressure cooker for about 1 hour) until the meat is very tender and easily shreds.
7. Remove the meat from the cooking liquid and shred it with two forks. Reserve the cooking liquid (consomé).
8. Skim excess fat from the top of the consomé and save it for frying the tacos.
9. Dip each corn tortilla lightly into the reserved fat and place it on a hot skillet.
10. Add shredded beef and cheese to one half of the tortilla. Fold and cook until crispy and golden on both sides.
11. Repeat with the remaining tortillas and filling.
12. Serve the tacos hot with chopped onion, cilantro, lime wedges, and a bowl of warm consomé for dipping.

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve with Mexican rice and refried beans.
- Garnish with extra cilantro, diced onion, and fresh lime juice.
- Offer spicy salsa or pickled jalapeños for additional heat.
- Serve the warm consomé alongside the tacos for dipping.
