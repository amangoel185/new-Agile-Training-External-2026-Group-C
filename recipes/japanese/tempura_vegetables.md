# Tempura Vegetables

- Cuisine Type: Japanese
- Preparation Time: 10 minutes
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes
- Serving Size: 4

## Ingredients

- 100g (approx) each of a mix of firm vegetables cut into bite-size pieces;
  aubergine, broccoli, courgette, mushrooms, red pepper and sweet potatoes
  
- tempura batter

    - groundnut or sunflower oil for deep frying

- sauce
    - 3 tbsp soy sauce
    - 3 tbsp dry sherry
    - 1 tbsp brown sugar
    - 1 whole lemon's zest

## Method 

step 1
1. Heat the oven to 150C/fan 130C/gas. 
2. Mix together the sauce ingredients in a small bowl. Make the batter. 
3. Cover a baking tray with sheets of kitchen paper. 
4. Start to heat a deep-fat frying pan or large wok a third full of oil and have the frying basket, or slotted spoon to hand.

step 2
1. Use a thermometer to accurately measure the temperature of the oil.
2. when the temperature reaches 190C, lightly dip some of the prepared vegetables into the batter; shake off any excess.
3. Gently drop a few vegetable pieces into the hot oil. Be careful to avoid splashes of hot fat. 
4. Fry for about 2 mins until light golden in colour and crisp; then drain on kitchen paper.
5. Keep the tempura warm in the oven, leaving the door slightly ajar so that they stay crisp.

step 3
1. Repeat step 2 with the remaining vegetables.
2. Ensure the temperature of the oil reaches 190C for each batch.
3. Serve immediately on a warm plate with the dipping sauce.
